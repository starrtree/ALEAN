HOW TO PLAY

MOVE

SPACE / W / ↑ — short, precise hover taps. Ground contact does not trap you; you can launch again immediately.

PURPLE LASER

F by default — fast precision shot. A direct kill flashes the police UFO purple before it breaks apart. Remap it in Settings.

NEUROVINE BOMB

E by default — gravity-driven biological bomb. A hit grows green vines through the target, detonates, and chain-destroys nearby UFOs. Remap it in Settings.

STARRDRIVE

SHIFT / X — spend 50 boost energy for speed, invulnerability and x2 scoring. Near-misses and kills recharge it.

HEARTS

You begin with 3. Every 3 police UFOs destroyed awards an extra heart, up to the life cap.

POWER-UPS

Phase Shield, Police Jammer, Gravity Snare, Laser Hypercharge, Repair Heart, StarrSeed and Boost Cell.

5-STAR PURSUIT

The chase starts light. Each GTA-style star raises police density, fire rate, enemy classes, and pressure.

MOTHERSHIP BOSS

Deep into the run the camera pulls back, gravity shuts off, and the game becomes a free-flight arena. Use WASD / arrows to fly in any direction while police drones attack from every side.
